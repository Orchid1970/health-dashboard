Compound Detail & Benefits
RetatrutideHuman RCT
4 mg · 2×/week (8 mg/wk) · 4:30 AM fasted
Triple GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon agonist — fat loss and appetite regulation. Phase 2 data, NEJM 2023.
CJC-1295 (no DAC)Limited Human
100 mcg · daily · 4:30 AM fasted
GHRH analog driving a pulsatile GH release into IGF-1. Human PK exists for the DAC form; no-DAC half-life figures are widely quoted but not primary-sourced.
IpamorelinHuman Safety
200–300 mcg · daily · 4:30 AM fasted
Selective GHS-R agonist — amplifies the GH pulse without the prolactin or cortisol rise seen with older secretagogues.
ARA-290 (Cibinetide)Small Human Trial
Per vial label · daily · 4:30 AM fasted
Innate repair receptor agonist — neuropathic and anti-inflammatory signaling. Small Phase 2 data.
AOD-9604RCT — Failed Phase 2b
300 mcg · daily · 4:30 AM fasted
Intended lipolysis without GH's glucose effects. Six RCTs showed modest results but the 24-week Phase 2b missed its endpoint and the program was shelved. Flagged for removal.
SS-31 (Elamipretide)Human RCT
5–10 mg · Mon/Wed/Fri · 7:30 AM post-training
Stabilizes cardiolipin on the inner mitochondrial membrane — improves electron transport and reduces ROS leak.
KLOW (BPC-157 · TB-500 · GHK-Cu · KPV)Animal / Preclinical
Per vial label · daily · 7:30 AM post-training
Tissue repair, angiogenesis and collagen signaling. GHK-Cu is copper-dependent — relevant with serum copper at 75 µg/dL. Human injectable data is thin; most evidence is animal.
SemaxRegional Human Data
Per vial label · 7:30 AM post-training
Nootropic, BDNF-linked. Clinical use and literature are largely Russian.
SelankRegional Human Data
Per vial label · 7:30 AM post-training
Anxiolytic via GABA / serotonin modulation. Clinical use and literature are largely Russian.
NAD+Mechanism Only
Per vial label · daily · 8:00 AM with first meal
Sirtuin substrate, shifts the NAD+/NADH ratio. Injectable human RCTs are absent — benefit is extrapolated from oral precursor trials. Requires a slow 10–15 min push to limit flushing and nausea.
GlutathioneLimited Human
Per vial label · Mon/Wed/Fri · 8:00 AM with first meal
Master antioxidant and phase II detox support. Injectable human data is limited. Keep 30–60 min and a different site from GHK-Cu — co-formulating strips the copper.
DSIPWeak Human Evidence
100–300 mcg · Sun–Thu · 8:30 PM pre-sleep
Intended to deepen slow-wave sleep. Human literature is thin and contradictory — a small 1981 study was positive, a 1992 double-blind study was not.
Evidence Tiered · Doses Verified · 4 Blocks · 12 Compounds · AOD-9604 Flagged
Evidence tiers are shown on purpose. Only Retatrutide and SS-31 sit on positive human randomized data. KLOW is animal / preclinical. Injectable NAD+ is mechanism-extrapolated from oral precursor trials. DSIP's human literature is thin and contradictory. AOD-9604 is kept visible rather than deleted — its Phase 2b missed endpoint earns the red flag, but all protocol changes are on hold until the remaining labs land. Doses appear only where they have actually been set; “per vial label” means no figure has been established.

Walk Block — Protected
465 min/week · HR 71–96 · intensity 26–45 · mostly zone 0
Non-negotiable connection time with Alma and the dog. This is roughly 465 minutes a week of zone-0 NEAT and it is a primary driver of visceral fat at 4.5 and vascular age at 55.3. The lift change does not touch it — walk volume is held, not traded.
Lift Block — Scaling Up
80 → 135 min/week · 3 × 25 min → 3 × 45 min · intensity 50–56 · HR 102–111
Session frequency is already right at three days a week — only duration changes. Going from about 25 minutes to 45 raises weekly resistance volume roughly 69% and pulls the aerobic-to-resistance ratio from 6.8:1 toward 4.0:1. Weekly set volume is the variable most tightly tied to hypertrophy.
Walk Protected · Lift 3 × 45 · Ratio Target 4.0 : 1 · Fasted Block ~105 min
Fuel flag. Walk plus a 45-minute lift is roughly 105 minutes of fasted training in one block. The nocturnal floor has recovered to 78–91 mg/dL, but that is still a thin tank for a resistance session at the tail end of it. Check the CGM mid-lift for the first two weeks and keep 10–15 g of fast carbs within reach. A sub-70 reading mid-session is the signal to add a small pre-lift carb — not to shorten the session.
